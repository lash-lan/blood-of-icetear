# Nilrealm at the Dragon Peaks, as prompts

Five scenes, after the Divine Departure, in the Dragon Peaks of the Western
Continent. He has lost most of his divinity, his mother is gone, and he has
started walking the world. These are the first people he goes to.

**Each block is complete on its own.** Master style, the cast, the scene and
the negative prompt are all inside it.

The master style is **the same oil painting used in the early life set**, so
the story holds together. Your references are more rendered than that, so if
you would rather these look like the references, say so and I will swap the
medium across both sets rather than let them drift apart.

## Questions, before you spend attempts

**Tiamel's father still needs a name.** You have settled that she is
Tiamatra's granddaughter, so the bible now runs her line through Tiamatra's
own son, with Galatrel still her mother and all the Hansall material intact.
The one loose end is that the earlier draft called her father Osiris, and
Osiris is her great uncle, so he cannot keep it unless he was named after
him deliberately.

**The prison holds more than Bahamut.** The archive only records Bahamut sealed in the south-west of the Western Continent under Tiamatra's enchantment, which cost her ninety percent of herself to hold shut. You said black dragons, plural, so I have written several. If it is Bahamut alone, the scene becomes one enormous bound shape rather than many and I will rewrite it.

**Does Tiamatra know he is her brother?** He is the sixth child and nobody was told. I have written the first scene as though she knows and he is expected. If she does not, it is a stranger walking into her hall claiming kinship, which is a completely different picture and a better one.

**I read "feathered court" as her feathered coat.** Fully black robes with his mother's cloak over them. Say if you meant an actual court of feathered attendants.

**The style is the early life oil painting, not your references.** Your references are more rendered and cinematic. I kept the medium consistent so the story reads as one thing. If you want the rendered look, I will swap it across both sets together rather than let the early life and this drift apart.

# The life of Nilrealm

Everything the archive holds about him, in the order it happened, so there is
one place to track it and add to it.

**Each moment is numbered.** When we come to make the pictures, one prompt per
number, so nothing drifts. Add a moment anywhere and we can renumber.

Sources are the world bible, the Drive documents, and the canon Lash settled
in October 2026. **Anything still open is listed at the end rather than
guessed at here.**

---

## I. Before he existed

**1. Ra goes looking for knowledge he does not have.**
The deepest of it is not in his own realm, so he enters Mór-Ríoghain's.

**2. They make something neither of them intended.**
What happens there takes eons. They grow close enough to make a life, and the
life takes root inside her.

**3. Ra goes home, and does not know.**
Mór-Ríoghain does. She says nothing, and carries it for eons more.

---

## II. Born in the Frozen North

**4. She gives birth as her Avatar, not as herself.**
In mortal scale, beneath the Great World Tree in the Frozen North. This one
decision explains everything odd about him.

**5. Most of him never comes through.**
A great weight of his essence and power stays behind in her realm, and is
still there. What walks the world is the part that fit.

**6. The Avatar raises him under the Tree.**

**7. He meets his true mother only in sleep.**
In her realm, which is the one place he has ever been whole. This is why he
becomes a dragon of dreams rather than a dragon of fire. Sleep is the door
home.

**8. He takes after her, not his father.**
Where his five siblings stand with Ra and take the shapes Ra's children take,
he prefers the prophet and the sorcerer. The five are powers. He is a mind.

---

## III. The parting

**9. The Law of Divine Departure. He stays.**
He could have returned to Ra's pocket realms at full strength and refuses,
the way Tiamatra refused. He loses most of his divinity for it.

**10. The Avatar leaves.**
Afterwards he takes up her black feathered cloak, and wears it among mortals
ever after. Before she goes he is in white. After, her feathers.

---

## IV. The three things he carries

**11. The dragon-scale tome.**
Gold and white, made from his own shed scales at the World Tree's suggestion,
and kept as the journal of his travels.

**12. The white phoenix feather fan, of seven feathers.**
From the Great White Phoenix of the Fae Lake, which recognised him on sight.
It controls white phoenix flame that can both burn and heal, and the winds of
Gehenna, which strike with the force of a thousand tidal waves or condense
into one of the sharpest blades in the world.

**13. The White World Staff.**
Sprouted for him by the Great White World Tree after a week of meditation and
prayer, from a willing root. The branch was never severed. It chose to leave.

---

## V. The pilgrimage to the peak of the world

**14. The undead kneel as he passes.**
The purest land is hidden behind scores of the undead, and as he crosses the
frozen North they separate and kneel, because in their core they knew what he
was, and even the ancient death magics respected the dragon of dreams.

**15. The lake parts and he walks.**
The pure land is cut off by an immense peak down which a legendary white
waterfall falls forever, and the water around it incinerates anything
corrupted. He chooses to walk the path rather than fly, and the great white
lake parts for him.

---

## VI. The long sleep

**16. Centuries in the liminal realm beneath the world.**
Where time does not pass but gathers, folding on itself, centuries drifting
through one another like snowflakes sinking into dark water. From there he
sees empires unborn, children crowned, old kings dying nameless in foreign
fields, swords never drawn, romances extinguished before their first meeting,
and wars fought only in the corridors of possibility.

**17. The one vision he keeps.**
A city rising from wilderness and ruin, towers gleaming in the first light of
dawn, its walls built not by one race or kingdom but by hands history had
taught to hate one another.

**18. He chooses his path and wakes into a world of strife.**

---

## VII. Walking the world

**19. He takes the visage of Nilrealm the White.**
A tall slender man of youthful half-elven beauty, long black hair, pale skin
untouched by age, and eyes that betray him, pupils narrow and draconic with
the reflections of ages moving inside them. He chooses that rather than
arriving as a mountain of scale and flame, which would have sent armies
running and temples collapsing under the weight of prophecy.

**20. He travels alone to understand the races, the powers and the conflicts.**

**21. Sal the Wanderer teaches him.**
A jolly old man, slim and fit, always moving and always writing. Over time he
realises Sal is far more than she lets on, that she is immortal, and he comes
to consider her the **first World Sage**. She is Salomei.

**22. He becomes the World Sage.**
The sixth and final rank of Nature, which is why the staff is his rank rather
than his decoration.

---

## VIII. The Age of Kingdoms, which is his age

**23. He gives pieces of himself away.**
Moving in his mortal guise across all three continents, he finds people who
are nothing yet and puts a fragment of himself into each one, a word, a
working, a debt, a thing they cannot name afterwards.

**24. Those people become houses, and the houses become powers.**
A great deal of the nobility of the Third Age is his doing, and almost none
of it knows.

**25. The old revere him and the young despise them for it.**
The bloodlines who remember him bow, be they kings, wealthy or powerful. Their
children and grandchildren watch them kneel and look at him with contempt.

---

## IX. The Nine and the White Kingdom

**26. He wakes in the frozen North and sends visions.**
To Azikeal and Erzakial Icetear, Princess Nyrial Dagrhan, Edmonta Lothrin,
Helen Ploki, Cholo Puto, Leon Godrick, Lycareon Tyberious and John Sedrick.

**27. The Nine meet in a clearing and immediately fight each other.**
Because the recognition between them was born not of meeting but of inherited
hatred.

**28. He shows them the white marble city, and preserves them in marble.**
Azikeal reaches for Erzakial as the stone covers his fingers. Nyrial never
looks away from Nilrealm as it rises across her face. And before the stone
takes him, Leon says: *"One day, Sage, your future will ask more of you than
you are willing to surrender."*

**29. Tyberious kneels in a broken cottage.**
He turns beneath a strike, guides the man down rather than throwing him, steps
away and lowers himself to one knee. Nilrealm reads him: suspicion, rage,
terror, compassion, and a will that had not yet learned how much suffering the
world could demand of it. *"Being willing to stand when no one else does. The
world notices such people. Then it asks them to stand again."* The first
covenant of Archavalon is made inside a broken cottage between exhausted
strangers.

**30. Knightguard is founded**, by Nilrealm and the White Knights, as a
sanctuary for all good races.

**31. The Nine wake to find Arthur White of Elderfen**, the golden haired
child.

---

## X. Camelot, and the long game

**32. He works through Merlin against Uther's anti-magic legacy.**
Teaching Arthur, by degrees, that magic itself is not the enemy. Uther
inherited that position from a state built on it four ages earlier, which
makes him a traditionalist rather than a fanatic, and makes the long game far
harder than it looks.

**33. He defeats Alice, as the secret wizard-servant.**
She flees to her duchy, too powerful to remove completely, and her lands
become a dark haunted border realm.

**34. Lycareon Tyberious goes undercover inside Morgana's coup**, secretly
loyal to Nilrealm throughout.

**35. He stands behind the Seven Knights of Determination**, without ever
taking a throne.

---

## XI. What he leaves

**36. The City of Light.**
The last bastion of the civilised races, holding elves, humans, dwarves,
beastkin and Fae who had spent four ages at each other's throats. **The first
real proof of his vision**, built by hands history had taught to hate one
another.

**37. One sacred scale, given away.**
It is forged into an ancient crystal together with the Light of Hyperion, a
relic reserved for the end of hope. Budz drives it into Lazorial's chest and
the mountain vanishes under a pillar of white fire.

**38. His theme, stated plainly.**
Against Nalshal the King of Fear, Arthur and Nilrealm are the counter
argument: **fear faced honestly becomes courage and compassion.**

---

## Still open

Listed rather than guessed at.

**The staff conflicts with the picture.** The bible says the White World Staff
was sprouted for him by the Great White World Tree after a week of meditation,
from a willing root that was never severed and chose to leave. The parting
scene has Mór-Ríoghain's Avatar holding it as a gift before she goes. Both are
good. They cannot both be how he got it. Either she is holding it for him
while he meditates, or the Tree story is replaced.

**When does the long sleep happen?** He is born, raised, parted from his
mother, gathers three objects and makes a pilgrimage, and he also sleeps for
centuries beneath the world. The order of moments 14 to 18 against 9 to 13 is
not settled anywhere.

**How many times has he slept?** The archive says he woke in the frozen North
to gather the Nine, and also that he woke into a world of strife and then
travelled alone for a long time learning the races. That reads like two
wakings, which would mean two sleeps.

**Where do the disguises sit?** He is recorded as "a half-elven servant's
companion, and a manservant's wizard", which places him beside Merlin in
Camelot, but there is no moment written for how that began.

**Does he know Cholo Puto betrays Arthur?** He reads people to the bone and he
chose all Nine. The traitor being the one who reads people best is a good
irony either way, but whether Nilrealm saw it coming changes his character
completely.

**Does he ever meet his five siblings?** Nothing in the archive says he does.

# Who fights, and how

Every prominent character in the archive with a combat role in plain class
terms. **Stated** means the bible says it outright. **Inferred** means I read it
off their actions, their weapon or what they founded. **Open** means nothing in
the archive tells us and you need to decide.

Roles are drawn from the world's own vocabulary where it has one, since
Archavalon already names Paladins, Rangers, Mage Knights, Wardens and Slayers.

## The Hemarc brothers

The clearest deliberate design in the archive: three brothers, three answers.

| Who | Role | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Landervale Hemarc** | **Dragon knight.** Grey Knight, then commander of the Dragon Core, then a **Slayer Angel.** Bound an Ancient Grey Dragon's heart and survived Nara | Stated |
| **Nadring Hemarc** | **Spellblade.** He did not join a class, **he invented one.** A slash becomes a fire spell, a parry becomes an arcane barrier, a thrust carries lightning. Founded the **Mage Knights** after both wizards and knights rejected him | Stated |
| **Dheivan Hemarc** | **Non-combatant**, and that is his arc. A model painter who avoids pressure. His redemption **requires soul synthesis with a Faelorn, which demands absolute trust**, from the one man who has never risked anything | Stated |

## What is missing

Three gaps worth filling, in order of how much they matter.

**Sedrick, the Grey King and Merlin are now settled** and their rows above are
stated rather than open.

What remains open is **Morgana**, who assembles twelve to resurrect the land
three hundred years after everyone else is dead. That reads as necromancy or as
deep druidry and the archive does not choose, which matters because Mordred, the
first of her twelve, is already a druid.

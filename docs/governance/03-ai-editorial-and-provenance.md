# AI, editorial and provenance policy

The rule the whole product stands on, and the machinery that enforces it.

---

## 1. The integrity rule

**Nothing about a real person is ever invented.** Not a quotation, a memory, a
date, an award, a qualification, a belief, a relationship or a number. Not a
plausible one. Not a placeholder to be corrected later.

Where something is not known, the Chronicle shows a marked gap that renders as
nothing until a human fills it. The family can see every gap. An unsourceable
fact does not ship, because there is no hedging sentence left to carry it.

**This is already enforced in the reference build.** It is the asset, not an
aspiration.

## 2. Source to story

No draft is written from an AI's own knowledge of the world. Five steps, in
order, and step four is never skipped.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Gather | Interviews, documents, photographs, recordings |
| 2. Fact register | Each fact with its source, date, confidence and permissions |
| 3. Draft | AI writes from the approved source material only |
| 4. **Human verification** | A person checks every date, name, quotation and claim against its source |
| 5. Family approval | They approve. Then it publishes. The evidence is kept |

## 3. The four kinds of statement

Every claim in a Chronicle is one of these, and they are never blurred.

| Kind | How it is written |
|---|---|
| **Verified fact** | Stated plainly. Source in the private record |
| **Personal recollection** | Attributed to the person who remembers it |
| **Disputed account** | Both accounts given, or neither. Never silently pick the better story |
| **Artistic reconstruction** | Labelled on the page as a reconstruction |

Where two relatives disagree, the more dramatic version is not the tiebreaker.

## 4. Images: what may be done, and what must be said

| Treatment | Rule |
|---|---|
| Colour correction, scratch removal | Keep the original. Record the edit |
| AI restoration or upscaling | Keep the original. A human reviews invented detail |
| Animating a photograph | Rights confirmed. Synthetic movement disclosed |
| Reconstructed historical scene | Labelled on the page as an artistic reconstruction |
| Synthetic image of a real person | Likeness authority required, plus prominent disclosure |
| Fabricated scene of real events | Not produced |

**The hard rule: an AI-generated image never becomes the evidence for the event
it depicts.** If a model draws a wedding in 1962, no visitor may mistake it for
a photograph of that wedding.

For every generated or altered asset, five things are kept: the original, the
generated version, the source information, the tool used, and who approved it.

## 5. What visitors are told

Plain sentences on the page, not buried in terms.

- "Narration uses a licensed synthetic voice."
- "This voice has been digitally recreated with the speaker's written
  authorisation. It is not an original recording."
- "This scene is an artistic reconstruction based on family recollections. It is
  not a photograph."
- "Historical information was reviewed and approved by the family. Some accounts
  are personal recollections."

**Verified 25 October 2026:** EU AI Act Article 50 has applied since 2 August
2026, covering provider and deployer transparency duties including disclosure of
certain synthetic audio, image and video content, with machine-readable marking
of pre-existing generative systems due by 2 December 2026. **Needs legal
confirmation:** whether a consenting person's own recreated voice, reading their
own approved words, falls inside the deepfake definition or an exception.
Disclose regardless. The sentence is free and the argument is not.

## 6. Tone

The AI does not sensationalise grief, trauma, illness or family conflict to make
a story land harder. A Chronicle is not a drama.

## 7. Interactive personas

A static Chronicle tells an approved story. An interactive persona generates new
statements nobody approved, and visitors will attribute them to the real person.

**Not offered.** If it is ever built, it is a source-grounded archive assistant
that answers only from documented material, identifies itself as AI, cites its
sources, admits uncertainty, refuses to invent memories, and never speaks in the
first person as the subject.

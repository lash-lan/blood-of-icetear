# The launch checklist

Twelve items. Ordered by what blocks what, not by how large each one is.

**The ordering rule:** three items block the first sale. The rest can be built
while selling. Governance is not permitted to become the next reason not to
launch.

## The pilot is already chosen

**The reference build is the pilot.** It is finished, it is live, it is about a
living family who participated, and it is currently running without the
paperwork. Bringing it into compliance proves the process on real material
instead of on invented demonstration data.

Four actions, in order:

| | Action | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add the disclosure line** for the recreated voices | A rule the business intends to sell is currently broken on its own reference |
| 2 | **Get Form B signed** by Yalindrani, Amma and Chammi for the recreations already published | Any use of the site as a sales asset |
| 3 | **Get Form A signed** by Chandima, and Form C from every other living person named, pictured or quoted | The same |
| 4 | **Decide the indexing question** deliberately, and record the decision | It is currently on by default, which nobody chose |

Also required, and separate: **written permission from the family to use their
Chronicle as a commercial reference.** Without it the main sales asset cannot be
shown to anyone.

## What is explicitly out of scope at launch

- Deceased-person voice recreation. Refused.
- Realistic synthetic likeness of a real person. Refused.
- Interactive memorial personas. Refused.

These are not pending features awaiting demand. They are declined.

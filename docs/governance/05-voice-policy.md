# Voice policy

The strongest controls in the company, because a convincing replica of a voice
can be used to impersonate a person, invent statements they never made, and
deceive the people who love them.

---

## The three tiers

| Tier | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Licensed synthetic narrator** | A generic, commercially licensed voice reads the Chronicle | **Standard. Included in every Chronicle** |
| **2. Consented recreation** | A living person authorises a synthetic version of their own voice | **Sold separately, under every control below** |
| **3. Deceased person's voice** | A family asks for narration in the voice of someone who has died | **Refused** |

## Why tier 3 is refused

A son saying "I am her son, I give permission" is emotionally understandable and
legally insufficient. It does not establish whether she wanted her voice
recreated, who owns the recordings, or whether other relatives object.

Refusing it is not abandoning a valuable feature. It is refusing to make the
first paying client the test case for the most sensitive problem in the field.

The position is reviewed only after a documented legal and ethical review, a
stronger consent framework, and a process for resolving competing family claims.
Until all three exist, the answer is no, at any price.

## The controls for tier 2

Every one is required. None is optional for a discount.

1. **Separate written authorisation.** Form B in file 02. Never a tick box
   inside a service agreement or website terms.
2. **Only the person themselves may sign.** Not a spouse, not a child, not the
   paying client.
3. **They make the recording themselves**, and listen to a sample of the result
   before approving it.
4. **Identity and authority verified**, and the method recorded.
5. **Script approval.** They approve the exact words before anything is
   published. A changed word means a new approval.
6. **Purpose limitation.** One Chronicle, one script. Never another client,
   never advertising, never a demonstration, never a portfolio sample.
7. **No open impersonation.** No visitor may type words for a real person's
   cloned voice to speak. This is absolute.
8. **Restricted storage.** Source recordings and voice models are Confidential
   under file 04: encrypted, administrator access only, no unauthorised
   download.
9. **Vendor checked.** Whether they store samples, train on them, permit
   deletion, and grant commercial rights. File 08.
10. **Visitor disclosure.** The page says the voice is a digital recreation and
    not an original recording. File 03.
11. **Withdrawal.** On written withdrawal, the narration comes down within [N]
    working days and the model and recording are destroyed.
12. **Logging.** Every generation, approval and publication is recorded, so
    misuse can be found and answered.

## Consent is necessary, not sufficient

A signature on Form B does not resolve:

- **Copyright in the source recording**, which may belong to whoever made it.
- **Performers' rights**, where the recording is of a performance.
- **Contractual restrictions** on an existing recording.
- **The privacy of other people** audible in the same recording.

Each is checked separately.

**Needs legal confirmation.** Protection for voices, likenesses and digital
replicas is handled through several overlapping areas of law rather than one
universal right, and the position differs by country. A qualified Malaysian
adviser must review this policy, and any client outside Malaysia raises the
question again.

## The existing recreations

Three living women's voices are already recreated and published on the reference
site: Yalindrani, and two family members recorded as Amma and Chammi. They
participated willingly, and Yalindrani recorded herself and chose between two
takes.

**They are not removed.** They are brought into compliance:

1. Form B signed by each of the three, covering the recreation already made.
2. The disclosure line added to the page.
3. The source recordings and models moved into Confidential storage with the
   retention rule applied.
4. The indexing decision taken deliberately.

That converts the reference build from an exposure into the first properly
governed Chronicle, and into the proof that the process works.

# The consent pack

Four forms. Nothing is collected, processed or published without the ones that
apply.

**Needs legal confirmation.** These are working drafts written to be usable
immediately and to be reviewed by a qualified Malaysian adviser before the first
paying commission. They are not legal advice.

**The governing idea:** consent is necessary and often not sufficient. Copyright
in a recording, a photographer's rights, a contract, and other people's privacy
are separate questions that a signature on one of these forms does not settle.

---

## Form A: Subject release
*For the person whose life is being recorded, while living.*

I, **[full name]**, agree that The Chronicle Company may prepare and publish a
Chronicle about my life.

Tick each separately. An unticked line is a refusal.

- [ ] **My story.** My life, career and family history may be written about.
- [ ] **My name.** I may be named.
- [ ] **My photographs.** The photographs listed in the attached schedule may be
      shown.
- [ ] **My words.** Letters, messages and statements I have written may be
      reproduced as I wrote them.
- [ ] **Publication.** The Chronicle may be published at a web address.
- [ ] **Search engines.** The Chronicle may be found through search engines.
      *(If this is not ticked, the Chronicle is published with indexing
      switched off.)*

I understand that:
- I will see and approve the full Chronicle before it is published.
- I may ask for anything to be changed or removed at any time.
- A request to remove something is acted on first and discussed afterwards.
- Nothing about me will be invented. Where something is not known, the Chronicle
  shows a gap rather than a guess.

Signature ............................  Date ..............

## Form B: Voice authorisation
*Separate from Form A. Never a tick box inside another document.*

**This form is only ever signed by the living person whose voice it is.**

I, **[full name]**, authorise The Chronicle Company to create and use a
synthetic recreation of my voice.

- [ ] I made the source recording myself, on [date].
- [ ] I have listened to a sample of the recreated voice and approve it.
- [ ] I approve the exact words it will speak, attached as a script.

**Limits of this authorisation.** The recreated voice may be used **only**:
- in the Chronicle named here, and nowhere else,
- to speak the approved script, and nothing else,
- until I withdraw this authorisation in writing.

It may **not** be used for any other Chronicle, any other client, any
advertising, any demonstration, or any statement I have not approved. No visitor
may type words for it to speak.

**Storage.** My source recording and the voice model are encrypted, reachable
only by named administrators, and destroyed on request or at the end of the
retention period in the data policy.

**Withdrawal.** If I withdraw this, the narration is removed from the Chronicle
within [N] working days, and the voice model and source recording are destroyed.

**Disclosure.** Visitors will be told that the voice is a digital recreation and
not an original recording.

Signature ............................  Date ..............
Identity verified by ....................  Method ..............

## Form C: Third-party permission
*For every other living person who appears by name, face or private words.*

The paying client cannot give this. The person themselves must.

I, **[full name]**, relationship to the subject **[.........]**, agree that the
Chronicle about **[subject]** may include:

- [ ] my name
- [ ] photographs showing me, as listed in the attached schedule
- [ ] words I wrote, reproduced as I wrote them
- [ ] a story about me, as drafted and attached

- [ ] I agree to it being publicly visible, **or**
- [ ] I agree only to it being visible to invited family.

I may withdraw this at any time, and the material is removed first and discussed
afterwards.

Signature ............................  Date ..............

## Form D: Guardian authorisation
*For anyone under 18 appearing by name or face.*

I, **[full name]**, as parent or guardian of **[child's name]**, aged **[ ]**,
agree that the Chronicle may include:

- [ ] the child's name
- [ ] photographs showing the child, as listed in the attached schedule

- [ ] Publicly visible, **or**
- [ ] Visible only to invited family. *(Recommended, and the default where the
      client expresses no preference.)*

I understand that The Chronicle Company will, on request after the child turns
18, review these permissions with them directly, and will act on their decision.

Signature ............................  Date ..............

## Where the deceased are concerned

No form here covers a dead person, because a dead person cannot sign one.

For a Chronicle about someone who has died, the following are recorded instead,
and none is a substitute for the others:

1. **Who is authorising**, their relationship, and on what basis.
2. **Whether other close relatives object**, having been asked.
3. **Any documented wish** the person expressed in life.
4. **Who owns the recordings, photographs and letters**, which is a separate
   question from who is related to them.
5. **The privacy of living people** who appear in the same material. Their
   consent is still required under Form C, and is not diminished by the
   subject's death.

**Voice recreation of a deceased person is refused.** See file 05.

---

## Storage of signed forms

Signed forms are **Confidential** under file 04: encrypted, administrator access
only, never in the website repository, never in any AI tool, and retained for as
long as the Chronicle is published plus the period in the retention schedule.

# The Chronicle Company: governance operating model

Adopted from the AI governance assessment of 25 October 2026. This file is the
map. Each numbered file beside it is one working part.

House rules apply here too: no em dashes, no semicolons in anything a client
reads.

---

## The three principles everything else serves

1. **Possession is not permission.** Someone handing you a photograph, a
   recording or a story does not make them able to authorise every use of it.
2. **Reconstruction is not reality.** AI may help tell a story. It may never
   manufacture historical evidence or put invented words in a real mouth.
3. **Preservation creates responsibility.** Promising to safeguard a family's
   history means being able to protect it, maintain it, export it, and
   eventually dispose of it properly.

## What the company is

**A human-led heritage preservation service, assisted by AI.** Not an AI system
that manufactures biographies. That is the honest description, it is the safer
one, and it is the reason a family pays a premium: they are buying care,
accuracy, discretion and stewardship.

## The four functions

| Function | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **Govern** | These policies, who approves what, the consent standard, the vendor rules |
| **Map** | Per project: who appears, what data, which AI tools, who is affected, which laws |
| **Measure** | Per project: privacy, accuracy, identity, security, copyright and emotional risk |
| **Manage** | Controls applied, release signed off, complaints monitored, incidents answered |

Every commissioned Chronicle passes a documented release gate before it is
published. File 06.

## The product phases, and what is out of scope

| Phase | Contains | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Controlled Chronicles** | Human-reviewed biography, client-approved photographs, licensed generic narration, public or private site, consent records, secure storage, exportable archive | **The commercial offer** |
| **2. Enhanced storytelling** | AI photograph restoration, animation, reconstructed scenes, and living-person voice recreation under full authorisation | **Sold only with the full Phase 2 controls in place** |
| **3. Sensitive identity features** | Deceased-person voice recreation, realistic synthetic likeness of a real person, interactive memorial personas | **Not offered. No exceptions, at any price** |

A deceased person's voice is not a pricing question. It is refused.

## What is asserted here, and what is not

This is an operating framework, not legal advice. Three categories are kept
apart throughout:

- **Policy**: a rule this company sets for itself. Binding internally.
- **Verified**: a fact checked against a public source on a stated date.
- **Needs legal confirmation**: flagged, and not relied upon until a qualified
  Malaysian adviser confirms it.

Anything in the third category is marked. Do not quote it to a client.

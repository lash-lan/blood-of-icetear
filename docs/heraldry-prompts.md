# Heraldry prompts

Sixteen marks, each prompt self-contained so it can be copied on its own.

**Why they all look the same at the start.** A sigil is not a painting. It has
the opposite failure mode: instead of over-rendering it, an image model will
bevel it, add a drop shadow, make it chrome, and hand you a corporate logo. Every
prompt below carries the same block of rules to stop that, so the set comes out
looking like one world rather than sixteen separate jobs.

**Ask for a transparent or plain flat background** and keep the file, because
these get reused on cards, page headers and banners.

## 1. Archavalon, the mark of the world

```
A single heraldic emblem, flat and graphic, painted by hand on cloth. One
straight vertical line running top to bottom, with eleven small marks set along
it at uneven intervals. The two marks at the very top and very bottom are larger
and are simple six-pointed stars. The nine between them are small simple trees,
each just a trunk and a crown, no more than a few strokes each. One of the nine
trees is bare and white with no crown at all. One other of the nine is drawn in a
sickly dark green-black and its shape is subtly wrong, leaning and malformed.
The other seven are whole.

Colours: a deep near-black field, the line and the whole trees in pale grey, the
burned tree in stark white, the corrupted tree in a dark rotten green.

Flat solid shapes, clean hard edges, no shading inside the shapes, no gradients,
no glow, no three dimensional effect, no metallic sheen. Slight irregularity in
the line as if painted by a human hand. Centred with generous empty space around
it.

Negative prompt: 3d render, bevel, emboss, drop shadow, glow, bloom, gradient,
chrome, metallic, glossy, photorealistic, logo mockup, corporate logo, vector
clipart, sparkles, speckle, texture overlay, filigree, crackle, busy detail,
realistic trees, landscape, text, letters, numbers, signature, watermark, border,
frame
```

## 7 to 10. The four Dark Houses

These four must look like four parts of one plant, so **generate them in one
image first** using the prompt below, then ask for each quarter separately. That
way the thorn, the spine, the bloom and the fall are drawn by the same hand.

```
Four heraldic emblems arranged in a two by two grid, flat and graphic, painted by
hand on cloth, all four clearly parts of the same single black plant.

Top left: a single long curved thorn, pointing downward.
Top right: a thin vertical stem with small sharp spines along it, pointing
downward, with one small white star set near the top of the stem.
Bottom left: a single closed flower bud hanging downward on a short stem, not
open.
Bottom right: a single leaf falling, drawn mid-fall, detached from anything.

Every element is drawn descending, hanging or falling downward. Nothing grows
upward. All four are the same matte black.

Colours: matte black shapes on a flat dark charcoal field, one single small white
star in the top right emblem only.

Flat solid shapes, clean hard edges, no shading inside the shapes, no gradients,
no glow, no three dimensional effect. Slight irregularity in the line as if
painted by a human hand. Each emblem centred in its quarter with generous empty
space around it, and no lines or borders dividing the grid.

Negative prompt: 3d render, bevel, emboss, drop shadow, glow, bloom, gradient,
chrome, metallic, glossy, photorealistic, logo mockup, corporate logo, vector
clipart, sparkles, speckle, texture overlay, filigree, crackle, busy detail,
upward growth, rising, blooming open flower, roses, realistic plant, botanical
illustration, grid lines, dividing borders, text, letters, numbers, signature,

```

## 11 to 15. The five Dragon Gods

No dragons. Each mark is the shape the element makes when it moves. Generate all
five together so they share a hand, then split them.

```
Five heraldic emblems in a row, flat and graphic, painted by hand on cloth. None
of them depict a creature. Each is a single abstract mark showing how one element
moves.

First: the path water takes, a long sinuous double curve, smooth and continuous,
narrowing at both ends.
Second: the way fire climbs, a cluster of upward tapering tongues of unequal
height, the tallest in the centre, all leaning the same way.
Third: what wind does to grass, a band of many parallel strokes all bent in one
direction by the same force, denser in the middle.
Fourth: how stone fractures, a single heavy angular shape split by three straight
branching cracks that do not meet.
Fifth: the way a feather falls, one long slow spiral arc drawn as a single
tapering line.

All five are the same weight and the same scale as one another, so they read as a
set.

Colours: all five in off-white on a flat deep indigo field.

Flat solid shapes, clean hard edges, no shading inside the shapes, no gradients,
no glow, no three dimensional effect. Slight irregularity in the line as if
painted by a human hand. Even spacing, generous empty space around each.

Negative prompt: 3d render, bevel, emboss, drop shadow, glow, bloom, gradient,
chrome, metallic, glossy, photorealistic, logo mockup, corporate logo, icon set,
app icons, vector clipart, sparkles, speckle, texture overlay, dragons, creatures,
animals, eyes, wings, faces, realistic fire, realistic water, landscape, text,
letters, numbers, signature, watermark, border, frame
```

## 16. The six, and the gap

This is the one that has to be arranged rather than drawn. The sixth Dragon God
is the space.

```
Six positions arranged evenly around a circle, flat and graphic, painted by hand
on cloth. Five of the six positions hold a simple abstract mark in off-white: a
sinuous double curve, a cluster of upward tongues, a band of bent parallel
strokes, an angular cracked shape, and a long tapering spiral.

The sixth position, at the top of the circle, is empty. Nothing is drawn there at
all. The five marks are placed so that the empty position is obvious and the
arrangement is clearly incomplete.

Colours: off-white marks on a flat deep indigo field. The empty position is the
same flat indigo as the rest of the field, with no outline, no dotted shape, no
placeholder of any kind.

Flat solid shapes, clean hard edges, no shading inside the shapes, no gradients,
no glow, no three dimensional effect. Slight irregularity in the line as if
painted by a human hand. No circle is drawn, only the arrangement implies one.

Negative prompt: 3d render, bevel, emboss, drop shadow, glow, bloom, gradient,
chrome, metallic, glossy, photorealistic, logo mockup, corporate logo, icon set,
vector clipart, sparkles, speckle, drawn circle, ring, outline around the gap,
dotted outline, placeholder, question mark, sixth symbol, filling the gap, six
marks, dragons, creatures, text, letters, numbers, signature, watermark, border,
frame
```

## Still needing a design decision before a prompt can be written

The eleven human orders, Hearthwinter's eleven Elven Houses, Azaelion's eleven
families and the Prime Gods all need their individual charges chosen first. The
grammar fixes the shape and the colour for each, but somebody has to decide that
the Grey Knights take the Wall and the Night Sisters take whatever they take.

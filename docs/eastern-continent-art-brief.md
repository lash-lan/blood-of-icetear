# The Eastern Continent: what still needs to be drawn

## Where the codex actually stands

**All five children of Ra now exist in both forms**, beast and humanoid, in
`Character References/The Dragon Gods`. Ten images. The prompts worked.

What is still missing is everything around them. The Foundation Age page
carries 28 figures, 12 places and a set of relics, and **none of those twelve
places is in the East**. The only Eastern asset on disk is
`eastern-migration-lands.jpg`, which is not wired into the page. The ten god
images are also not in the codex yet, so the site still shows Ra with no
children.

Two jobs follow from that: get the ten into the codex, and draw the East
around them.

### The two worth one more pass

**Delphyne's humanoid form has arms.** The whole anatomical point of her line
is that wyvern forelimbs *are* wings, so hers should be too: membrane running
wrist to hip, no free hands, nothing carried because nothing can be. What came
back is a woman with wings behind her, which reads as an angel and loses the
lineage. Worth one reroll with the wing-arms stated harder.

**Bahamut's humanoid form does not read as enormous.** Lying prone with no
mortal clearly beside him, he could be any size. He is the one god where scale
was lost. Adding the small coated figure by his shoulder fixes it and ties him
to Tiamatra in the same stroke.

Everything else is done and should go straight into the codex.

## The thesis of the Eastern Continent, which decides everything below

The East was saved by Bahamut and does not know it.

He saw the Black Sea Angel's fleet when his siblings would not. He fought it
alone. He won. The winning corrupted him, and he is remembered as the monster
his siblings had to put down. The continent he saved does not even hold his
tomb, because Tiamatra guards him in the West.

Every picture below should serve that. A founding story told as a secret is
far stronger than another map of another kingdom, and no image of it exists
yet.

## Tier 1. The divine foundation

**1. Ryujin, true form.** ✅ Done.
**3. Bahamut as he became.** ✅ Done, one of the strongest of the ten.

**2. Bahamut as he was.** ❌ Still missing, and still the one that matters.
Black wings wide enough to obscure the sun, whole and glorious, Guardian of
Sacred Earth. If the audience never sees what he was, his fall is only a
monster design. You now have the corrupted image to fall *from*, which makes
this more urgent, not less.

**4. The Black Sea Angel.** ❌ Still missing. The enemy nobody remembers and
the thing he swallowed. It must not read as a dragon, a demon or a titan. It
came from outside your world and should look like it. Highest creative risk
and highest payoff of anything on this list.

**5. The sealing.** ❌ Still missing. Bahamut put under, Tiamatra choosing to
stay. One picture carrying both the War of the Five Bloodlines and her
sacrifice.

## Tier 2. The two bloodlines of the East

So Eastern dragons read as a distinct evolutionary branch and not as recolours.

**6. Eastern Dragons.** Ryujin's line. Long bodies, short limbs, no wings,
flight by magic. Shown as a group or a lineage plate, not a single beast.

**7. Earth Wyrms.** Bahamut's line. Wings gone, some lineages limbless. Their
anatomy is the visible evidence of his corruption, which makes them the most
story-bearing creature design you have. Worth showing a range: one tragic,
one neutral, one monstrous, since you established they are not uniformly evil.

## Tier 4. The land itself

**11. The Eastern Continent, establishing shot.** One wide landscape that

## Open questions that block art

I am flagging these rather than inventing answers.

**Osiris and Delphyne have no territory.** Your own table says "territorial
role to establish" for both. Until that is settled I cannot place them, and
two of Ra's five children stay homeless.

**Is there an Eastern equivalent of the Elu-Dragar?** The West has dragon-elves
descended from Tiamatra through Tiamal. If the East has a parallel people, they
need a name and a progenitor before they can be drawn. If it deliberately does
not, that absence is itself worth stating, and it makes the Elu-Dragar rarer.

**Where is Bahamut's prison?** He defended the East and Tiamatra guards him in
the West, so he was moved. Who carried him, and how far, is a picture in
itself. Amelia left the location deliberately unconfirmed.

**Tiamal's parentage.** The older manuscript names Osiris and Galatrel as
Tiamel's parents; the new line places her in Tiamatra's descent. This has to
be settled before any Elu-Dragar lineage plate is drawn, and it also decides
whether Osiris has a Western foothold after all.

## A note on consistency

Every one of these should be generated with your site's own art style as a
reference image, the way Bahamut and Tiamatra were, using
`BOI-animation/keyframes/clash-v2.png`. Otherwise the East will not look like
it belongs to the same world as the elves intro.

The craft rules in `docs/dragon-god-prompts.md` apply throughout: one named
light source, three colours, large areas with no detail, a readable
silhouette, a mortal in frame for scale, and none of the banned words.

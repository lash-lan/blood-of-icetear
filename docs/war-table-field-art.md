# Art for the War Table grounds

Ten prompts, two for each of the five places the board gets set up in.

**Every block below is complete on its own.** Size, camera, lighting rules,
style and the negative prompt are all inside it. Copy one block, paste it,
generate, move to the next. Nothing on this page needs to be read first.

Save the results into `public/images/fields/` with the name given above each
block. I will wire them up when they land.

One thing worth knowing, and then you can ignore the rest of this page: the
backdrop and the ground are **two different camera angles**, not one picture
cut in half. The backdrop is eye level looking out at the horizon. The ground
is straight down at the floor. The page stands one up behind the board and
lays the other flat under it.

## 1. `hollanheart-sky.png`

```
A painted fantasy landscape backdrop, 1600 pixels wide by 1250 tall, landscape format. The horizon line must sit exactly halfway down the image, at the vertical centre.

Scene: an enchanted deep green summer forest seen from eye level across a clearing. Above the horizon, a wall of enormous ancient trees receding into green gloom, a dim green aurora light bleeding through the canopy from behind, a faint veil of old magic hanging in the air between the trunks. Off to the left third, one tree far larger than all the rest. Below the horizon, plain dark mossy ground receding away into the distance, completely empty, nothing standing on it.

Colour: deep forest greens and near black, with one soft pale green light source.

Rules that matter more than the scene. Everything important must be in the top half of the image, because the bottom half will be covered up. Leave the centre of the image empty and place the large tree off to one side. Keep it dark, quiet, low contrast and desaturated, because this is scenery that sits behind a game board and small figures have to stay readable standing on it. No foreground at all, nothing close to camera, no branches or leaves framing the edges or corners. Painted with visible brushwork, not photographic.

Negative prompt: bright, high contrast, saturated, sunlit, sun shafts, lens flare, foreground objects, branches framing the shot, leaves in the corners, path, road, river, bridge, ruins, buildings, people, creatures, birds, centred focal point, busy detail, text, letters, watermark, signature, border, frame, vignette
```

## 2. `hollanheart-ground.png`

```
A painted ground texture, 1024 by 1024 pixels, square. Seen from directly overhead, looking straight down at the floor. This is a floor, not a landscape. No horizon, no sky, no perspective, no vanishing point.

Surface: an enchanted forest floor. Deep moss in several shades of green, scattered fallen leaves, a few old roots crossing the ground, small pale green glowing spores caught down in the moss.

Colour: dark and low contrast, deep greens and browns and near black.

Rules that matter more than the surface. Flat even light with no sun direction and no cast shadows, because the page adds its own lighting and baked shadows will fight it. Even all over, with the same density of detail in every corner and no path, no trail, no clearing, no stream and no focal point of any kind, because a nine by nine grid is drawn on top and anything with a direction in it will look like a mistake once the board turns. Texture rather than objects. Painted with visible brushwork, not photographic.

Negative prompt: perspective, horizon, sky, three quarter view, angled view, tilted view, sunlight, dappled light, cast shadows, bright patch, path, trail, clearing, stream, centred object, skull, bones, flowers, mushrooms in a ring, people, creatures, insects, text, letters, watermark, signature, border, frame, vignette, high contrast
```
